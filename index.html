A place with roots

A historic place in the heart of Limone

The story of Incontro is rooted in the history of Limone sul Garda. Set among the historic terraces of the village, this place connects the character of the past with the pleasure of Italian dining today. The lake, the mountains and the traditional architecture of Limone create a setting where history remains part of the experience — alongside good food, wine and hospitality.

The Cuisine

Italian cuisine with a taste of Lake Garda

At Hostaria Al Buongusto, Italian cooking meets the flavours of Lake Garda and the Mediterranean. The menu brings together lake fish and seafood, handmade pasta, carefully prepared meat dishes and seasonal ingredients. From freshwater fish and filled pasta to Fassona beef and delicate desserts, each plate is presented with a contemporary touch while keeping the character of Italian cuisine. A table made for taking your time, sharing a bottle of wine and discovering something new with every course.
