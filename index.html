A place with roots

From the lemon gardens of Limone to Al Buongusto

Hostaria Al Buongusto lives within the walls of an ancient limonaia — the traditional lemon gardens that shaped the identity of Limone sul Garda. Built into the terraces between the lake and the mountain, these historic gardens protected citrus trees through the winter and made Limone one of Europe's most distinctive lemon-growing villages. Today, the old stone walls, terraces and surrounding village continue that story in a new way: through food, wine and hospitality.

The Cuisine

Italian cuisine, shaped by Lake Garda

At Hostaria Al Buongusto, Italian cooking meets the flavours of Lake Garda and the Mediterranean. The menu brings together lake fish and seafood, handmade pasta, carefully prepared meat dishes and seasonal ingredients. From freshwater fish and filled pasta to Fassona beef and delicate desserts, each plate is presented with a contemporary touch while keeping the character of Italian cuisine. A table made for taking your time, sharing a bottle of wine and discovering something new with every course.
